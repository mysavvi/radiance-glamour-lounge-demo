Understanding Aesthetic Clinic Consultation

An Aesthetic Clinic Consultation is the foundational step before any advanced cosmetic procedure. During this highly personalized session, our expert practitioners carefully evaluate your skin, discuss your aesthetic goals, and review your medical history to ensure optimal safety and efficacy.

We believe in a bespoke approach rather than a one-size-fits-all solution. This consultation allows us to design a tailored treatment plan that addresses your unique concerns—whether that involves anti-aging solutions, skin rejuvenation, or structural enhancements.

Key Benefits

  • Personalized assessment of your skin type and aesthetic goals.
  • In-depth medical history review to guarantee treatment safety.
  • Creation of a bespoke, step-by-step treatment plan.
  • Clear explanation of realistic expectations and timelines.
  • Opportunity to ask any questions in a private, luxurious setting.

Suitable Candidates

Anyone considering advanced skin treatments, cosmetic injectables, or plasma pen procedures must undergo a consultation. It is suitable for all adult women looking to enhance their natural beauty safely.

Who Should Avoid

  • Individuals under the age of 18.
  • Those unwilling to disclose relevant medical histories.

The Treatment Journey

1. Consultation Process

During the consultation, you will be seated in our private clinic room. We will conduct a visual and tactile assessment of the target areas, discuss your medical background, current skincare routine, and ultimately build a roadmap for your aesthetic journey.

2. Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Welcome & Paperwork: Completion of medical history forms.
  2. Discussion: Exploring your concerns and desired outcomes.
  3. Assessment: Professional analysis of your skin and facial anatomy.
  4. Plan Creation: Developing a bespoke treatment strategy.
  5. Q&A: Addressing all your queries regarding downtime, costs, and risks.

Recovery & Downtime

There is no downtime associated with a consultation. You can return to your daily activities immediately.

Results Timeline

The result of this session is a clear, actionable treatment plan ready for implementation.

Maintenance

We recommend a review consultation annually or whenever your aesthetic goals change significantly.

Safety, Risks & Professional Aftercare

Potential Risks

As this is an assessment-only appointment, there are absolutely no physical risks or side effects.

Aftercare Advice

  • Review the provided treatment plan at home.
  • Follow any pre-treatment skincare advice given by the practitioner.
  • Contact the clinic if any new medical conditions arise before your treatment day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, in most cases, the consultation fee can be redeemed against the cost of your subsequent treatment if booked within a specified timeframe.

How long does the consultation take?

A thorough aesthetic consultation typically takes between 30 to 45 minutes.
