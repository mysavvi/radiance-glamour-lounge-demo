Understanding Jalupro

Jalupro is an injectable bio-revitalizer containing a potent blend of amino acids (Glycine, L-Proline, L-Leucine, L-Lysine) and hyaluronic acid. It acts as a powerful dermal bio-revitalizer, providing exactly the building blocks your skin's fibroblasts need to synthesize new collagen.

As we age, fibroblast activity decreases, leading to wrinkles and loss of tone. Jalupro reawakens these cells, making it an excellent treatment for photo-aging, anti-aging, and repairing acne scarring or stretch marks.

Key Benefits

  • Deeply hydrates the skin.
  • Reverses signs of photo-aging and sun damage.
  • Reduces the appearance of acne scars and stretch marks.
  • Prolongs the effects of other aesthetic treatments (like fillers).

Suitable Candidates

Suitable for anyone noticing early signs of aging, dull skin, or wishing to improve the texture of scars.

Who Should Avoid

  • Active acne or infections in the treatment area.
  • Pregnant or nursing women.

The Treatment Journey

1. Step-by-Step Procedure

  1. Numbing: Application of anesthetic cream.
  2. Injection: Superficial micro-injections across the face, neck, or décolletage.

Recovery

Minimal. Slight redness and small raised bumps (papules) at the injection sites usually disappear within 24 hours.

Results

Requires a course of 3-4 treatments. Skin appears more hydrated immediately, with structural improvements over 4 weeks.

Maintenance

Maintenance every 6 months ensures lasting skin health.

Risks

Temporary bruising and redness.

Aftercare

  • Keep the skin clean and avoid makeup for 12 hours.
  • Avoid intense sun exposure.

FAQs

What is the difference between Jalupro and Profhilo?

While both are skin boosters, Jalupro specifically delivers amino acids directly to the fibroblasts to aggressively stimulate collagen, whereas Profhilo focuses heavily on deep hydration and tissue remodeling via pure HA.
