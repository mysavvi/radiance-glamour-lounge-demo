Frequently Asked Questions

Is Radiance Glamour Lounge strictly ladies-only?

Yes. We maintain a strict ladies-only policy across all salon and clinic services. Our staff is entirely female, and our treatment rooms provide total privacy.

Where is the salon located and what parking is available?

We are located at 12-16 Prince's Street in Merseyway Shopping Centre, Stockport (SK1 1SE). The Merseyway multi-storey car park is located right next door, and Stockport Train Station is just a 5-minute walk away.

Do I need a consultation before aesthetic treatments?

Yes. For anti-wrinkle injections, dermal fillers, microneedling, PRP, or laser hair removal, we carry out a full consultation to review your medical profile, discuss expectations, and explain post-treatment care.

Can I book multiple services in a single appointment?

Absolutely. Many of our clients combine a hair cut & color appointment with a facial, brow shape, or nail treatment during the same visit.
