# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Clients booking beauty, spa, aesthetics, and glamour lounge appointments online or in-clinic.

## Product Purpose
Radiance Glamour Lounge booking system & client portal for selecting treatments, choosing staff/time slots, and confirming appointments effortlessly.

## Brand Personality
Sophisticated, warm, luxurious, confident, pristine, modern luxury salon & clinic experience.

## Anti-references
SaaS-cliché dashboards, cheap generic templates, cluttered form inputs, loud neon accents, unstyled default browser controls.

## Design Principles
1. Tactile Elegance — Subtle neumorphic elevation, refined shadows, and soft ambient depth.
2. Effortless Flow — Step-by-step clarity with high contrast, legible labels, and zero friction.
3. Warm Luxury — Rich charcoal base paired with warm coral-rose accents (#ff7043) and elegant serif headings.
4. Precision Alignment — Balanced layouts, consistent 44px+ touch targets, and responsive spacing.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, minimum 4.5:1 text contrast ratio, clear focus rings, keyboard navigability, and prefers-reduced-motion support.
